# Sound credits

All sounds are synthesized from noise and sine waves by `tools/make_audio.py`
(no recorded samples). Released under CC0 1.0 by the site's author.

| file  | what it is                              |
|-------|-----------------------------------------|
| river | brown-noise river murmur, 20 s loop     |
| surf  | pink-noise surf, 3 waves per 24 s loop  |
| swish | band-passed noise swish, one-shot       |
| plop  | rising sine bubble, one-shot            |

To replace a sound with a recording, keep the file name and length and record
the source and licence here.
